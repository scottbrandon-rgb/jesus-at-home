# MAIN IDEA

- We had no midweek service this week, but this guide is made for tonight, around the table or on the couch as you celebrate a time of thanksgiving with your family. My challenge for all of us today is that we would not just practice giving thanks on Thanksgiving Day, but that giving thanks would become part of our daily rhythm.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

- When circumstances were not in his favor, Daniel still gave thanks to God. But here's the key: Gratitude was Daniel's response because it was already his daily rhythm. 

	➡ 1. **Gratitude Should Always Be Our Response** (Daniel 6:10)
	- "When Daniel knew that the document had been signed, he went to his house where he had windows in his upper chamber open toward Jerusalem. He got down on his knees three times a day and prayed and gave thanks before his God,"

	➡ 2. **Gratitude Should Be Our Daily Rhythm** (Daniel 6:10)
	- "...as he had done previously."

## THIS WEEK’S QUICK WIN

- Ask: When circumstances are not in your favor, what is your response?
- Go around the table or the living room and have everyone finish the sentence out loud: "God, thank You for..."
- Pray together and give thanks to God as a family.

## FURTHER DISCUSSION

- Have someone retell Daniel 6 in their own words.

## WHAT THIS SHOWS US ABOUT GOD

- **God is still good even when our circumstances are not.** Daniel's gratitude was not based on the news. It was based on who God is.
- **God is faithful to those who trust Him.** Daniel kept his eyes on the Lord and the Lord shut the mouths of the lions (Daniel 6:22).

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR US

 - Daniel's example reminds us of 2 important principles on this Thanksgiving Day:

	➡ 1. **GRATITUDE IS MY RESPONSE**
	- The news did not determine Daniel's attitude. His God did. We can't always choose what happens to us. But we can choose how we respond.

	➡ 2. **GRATITUDE IS MY RHYTHM**
	- Daniel didn't have to conjure up something in the midst of bad news. He continued to do what he had always done - pray and give thanks to God.
	- Your rhythm today will determine your response tomorrow.

## SELF

- **Reflect personally:**
    ‣ When the news isn't in my favor, do I turn to gratitude or grumbling?
	‣ Is thanking God a habit for me, or something I only do when things are going good?
	
- **What would it look like this week to:**
	‣ Pick one set time each day to give thanks to God. Examples: when you get up in the morning, at lunch time, before bedtime. And stick with this habit even after November.

## PRAYER

- God, thank You that You are good no matter what news comes our way. Thank You for our family, for the food on our table, and for Your constant faithfulness this year. Help us to be like Daniel, people who give You thanks on the good days and on the hard days. Let gratitude become part of our normal rhythm in this home. By Your grace and in Your strength, we choose gratitude over grumble. In Jesus' name, amen.
